line 01 — mission

buildFor(realWorld)

What kind of real-world problems do you want to solve with technology?

I like the problems that don't have clean textbook answers — the ones where the constraints are messy and the stakes are real. I want to build software that stays reliable under pressure: systems that keep working when the network drops, the data is imperfect, and the user just needs the thing to "do its job." Good engineering, to me, is quiet — you only notice it when it fails, so my goal is to make sure it doesn't.

line 06 — git log --first-year

adviceTo(firstYearMe)

If you could advise your first-year self, what would you say?

Ship things before you feel ready. The half-finished, slightly embarrassing projects teach you more than any perfectly-planned one you never start. Ask questions early instead of stalling in silence, and don't be afraid to build the ambitious version — the safe one is the one you'll forget.
